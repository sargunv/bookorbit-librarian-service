How changes and completeness are counted

Major update: at least three bibliographic fields changed, or a cover replacement plus a bibliographic change. Provider links are counted separately. “No metadata change” includes file-only organization.

Completeness counts nine populated fields: title, authors, description, language, publisher, publication date/year, page count, ISBN and genres. Optional series/subtitle and provider links are excluded. It measures missing data, not quality. Counts above use each job’s latest outcome; the log also retains retries. Before/after values come from saved pre-write records and selected patches; planned entries are not counted as applied changes.

Thumbnails are current BookOrbit covers unless labeled Before/After. Historical images from before capture was enabled are unavailable.
